1. Executive Summary

Sentinel / FamilyGuard is a parental-control application designed to allow a parent or legal guardian to connect a parent device with a child device, review child-device activity, manage app access, apply screen-time limits, and view child-device location where location permission is granted.

The application processes sensitive child-device information as part of its core parental-control purpose. The codebase supports collection and processing of account details, device identifiers, installed-app information, app usage data, child-device location, QR pairing data, local session data, and parental-control settings. The application uses Firebase, Appwrite, Google Maps, Google Play Services Location, CameraX, ZXing, and related Android platform services to deliver these features.

This document is structured for Google Play submission, enterprise review, investor diligence, public website publication, and legal counsel review. It avoids unsupported claims and includes placeholders where publisher-specific information must be completed.

2. Privacy Policy

2.1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how [Insert Legal Entity Name] ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when users access or use Sentinel / FamilyGuard (the "App"). The App is a parental-control service intended to be configured and used by a parent or legal guardian to supervise and manage a child device.

By creating a parent account, pairing a child device, enabling monitoring permissions, or using the App, the parent or legal guardian confirms that they have the authority to configure parental controls for the relevant child device and to consent to the processing described in this Privacy Policy.

2.2 Information We Collect

We collect information required to provide parental-control functionality. The categories supported by the codebase include:

  • Account information: parent name or username, email address, phone number, user type, Firebase or Appwrite identifiers, and account timestamps.
  • Child and device information: child name, parent name, Android ID, device name, device model, manufacturer, connection state, QR pairing information, and parent-child connection records.
  • Installed app information: app names, package names, app icons, app categories, system-app status, app counts, and update timestamps.
  • Usage and activity information: foreground app activity, app usage duration, screen-time totals, daily and weekly summaries, usage categories, active timers, app limits, blocked-app status, and related timestamps.
  • Location information: child-device latitude, longitude, accuracy, GPS/location status, and timestamps when location permission is granted and location services are available.
  • Permission, device, and service status information: app install/uninstall events, permission-related events, device status, heartbeat values, logout or removal commands, and service operation data.
  • Authentication and pairing information: QR pairing identifiers, share keys, OTP email data, and authentication state.
  • Local session information: login state, account role, user ID, email or phone, child device ID, parent ID/name, QR share key, connection status, and related local preferences.

2.3 How We Use Information

We use collected information to provide, secure, and maintain the App, including to:

  • Authenticate parent users and manage app sessions.
  • Pair parent and child devices using QR codes.
  • Display installed apps from the child device to the connected parent.
  • Calculate and present screen-time and app-usage reports.
  • Apply app blocking, focus modes, timers, and daily usage limits.
  • Display child-device location to the connected parent when location permission is enabled.
  • Maintain device connection status, service reliability, and synchronization.
  • Send OTP emails for verification or recovery through Appwrite-backed functions.
  • Detect app installation or removal events relevant to parental monitoring.
  • Help protect the App from unauthorized removal or tampering where Device Admin and related protections are enabled.

We do not use child-device information for behavioral advertising. No advertising SDK was detected in the reviewed codebase.

2.4 Local and Remote Storage

The App stores certain data locally on the device using Android app-private storage and SharedPreferences. Local data includes session state, user role, user identifiers, device pairing details, blocked-app settings, timers, app limits, and connection flags.

The App also stores data remotely through Firebase and Appwrite services. Remote data may include account records, parent-child connection data, installed-app lists, app usage summaries, child-device location, app-blocking commands, timers, device status, QR pairing data, and OTP/email-function data.

2.5 Data Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell personal information. We do not share child information with advertisers. We share information only as necessary to operate the App, including with infrastructure and service providers such as Firebase, Appwrite, Google Maps, and Google Play Services Location.

Child-device information is also made available to the connected parent account or parent device as a core feature of the App. This parent-child data flow is essential to parental-control functionality.

2.6 International Data Transfers

Service providers used by the App may process data on servers located in countries other than the country where the user resides. Where applicable, we rely on appropriate contractual and technical measures provided by our service providers to support lawful data transfers.

2.7 Security

The App declares HTTPS-only network configuration and disables cleartext traffic. Data transmitted to supported backend services is expected to use encrypted network connections. Local app data is stored within app-private storage protected by the Android operating system. No security measure is perfect, and users should maintain device-level protections such as screen locks and operating-system updates.

4. Accessibility Service Disclosure

Sentinel / FamilyGuard uses Android Accessibility Service exclusively to support parental-control and app-blocking functionality on a child device. The service helps detect the currently visible app or window, enforce parent-selected restrictions, and prevent unauthorized tampering with protection settings where applicable.

The Accessibility Service may:

  • Receive window and view-focus events.
  • Identify foreground apps and visible windows.
  • Inspect limited screen text in settings contexts to detect attempts to disable protection.
  • Return the device to the home screen when a blocked app is opened.
  • Support multi-window, floating-window, and app-blocking enforcement.

The Accessibility Service is not used to collect passwords, payment information, private messages, or unrelated personal content. Accessibility-derived information is used for app blocking, timer enforcement, device-protection workflows, and parental-control reliability.

The App is designed for use by parents or legal guardians. A parent or guardian must authorize setup and monitoring on the child device, including sensitive permissions such as Accessibility, Usage Access, Location, Camera, Notifications, and Device Admin where enabled.

8. Retention, Access, and Deletion

We retain account, device, usage, connection, location, and parental-control data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain connected devices, enforce parent-selected settings, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service.

Local data may remain on a device until the user logs out, clears app data, uninstalls the App, disconnects a device, or the data is otherwise cleared by app logic. Remote data may remain in Firebase or Appwrite until deleted by app workflows or upon verified request.
